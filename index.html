1 How it works

  1. Guess Freya's top 3 breeds and a percentage for each (they should add up to ≤ 100% — mixed pups leave room for “other”).
  2. Pledge an amount you're happy to donate. No card form here — use Venmo or PayPal below (or settle up with Colton after).
  3. When the DNA report lands, we score every guess. Closest wins bragging rights; the pledged pot goes to Charity TBD.
Scoring (simple & fair): For each breed that appears in both your guess and the DNA results, you earn min(your %, DNA %). Sum those across matching breeds. Highest score wins. Ties split the glory (and the pot still goes to charity). Example: you guess Lab 40 / Shepherd 30 / Husky 20; DNA is Lab 35 / Shepherd 25 / Poodle 20 → score = 35 + 25 = 60.
